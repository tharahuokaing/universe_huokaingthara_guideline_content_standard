STD-01 • PRODUCTION QUALITY BENCHMARK

Every video, film, music video, and entertainment clip maintains crisp visuals, balanced audio, and smooth narrative pacing.

STD-02 • BILINGUAL & UI REQUIRED

Khmer-English Accuracy & Interface Consistency
